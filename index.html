AI is making life-altering decisions in safety-critical industries.

Nobody is accountable for any of them.

Project Cardinal is building the governance infrastructure AI accountability has never had — starting with the industries where getting it wrong has a body count.

We are not a compliance tool. We are not a framework. We are the independent third-party infrastructure — a Standards Body, a commercial platform, and a Certification Body — that safety-critical industries have needed since the moment AI entered them.

Our initial focus is aerospace and defense, where NDAA Sections 1512 and 1513 create a June 2026 compliance deadline and no independent certification infrastructure exists to meet it.
